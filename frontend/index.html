ℹ️ About VocalTrace
Restoring Digital Trust in an Era of Synthetic Media

VocalTrace is an accessible, real-time acoustic forensics platform designed to safeguard individuals, families, and businesses against generative AI voice-cloning scams. With modern generative tools capable of mimicking speech patterns from just seconds of raw audio, traditional auditory perception is no longer enough to detect social engineering fraud. VocalTrace bridges this critical defense gap by tracing and flagging acoustic markers unique to synthetic media before irreversible decisions are made.

🎯 Our Mission
To democratize digital audio forensics by delivering an intuitive, low-latency verification system that shields everyday users from impersonation fraud, extortion schemes, and digital deception.
⚙️ How It Works
1. Audio Ingestion
Upload any standard voice note or recording (.wav or .mp3).
2. Forensic Acoustic Scan
The system inspects microscopic spectral anomalies—including neural vocoder phase shifts, artificial harmonic distributions, and robotic micro-tremors imperceptible to human ears.
3. Decisive Trust Rating
VocalTrace outputs an immediate 0–100% Authenticity Score paired with clear, non-technical guidance to assist users under high-pressure scenarios.
🛡️ Why It Matters
✓

✓
Frictionless & Lightweight: Operates directly inside a web browser without demanding enterprise forensics hardware.
✓
